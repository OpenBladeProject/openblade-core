# Tray artwork

The tray and generic-error ICO files are the approved negative-space artwork
from [openblade-branding](https://github.com/OSSBlade/openblade-branding),
generated by `tools/tray_geometry.py` and `tools/generate_brand_assets.py`.
The approved 2026-10-29 design centers the original Balanced S, restores the
smooth Performance curve, and uses double chevrons for HyperBoost. Power-source
glyphs have contour-following transparent clearance.

Copy only the `dark` and `light` files from `assets/brand/ico/tray` and
`assets/brand/ico/tray-status`. Each ICO contains 16, 20, 24, 32, and 48 pixel
frames. Light glyphs belong on a dark taskbar. Application, installer, and
notification artwork is separate; see [application artwork](APP-ASSETS.md).
