## Decided
- Native serves managed `reapplyChangedSettings` (protocol 90, no wire change) only with `service-thermal-convergence-v1`; other compositions keep the typed unsupported reply through `StatusRequest::serves`.
- Suppression now publishes Suppressed (Tombstoned 19 / BudgetExhausted 18, explicitReapplyRequired) instead of the D6 Paused/InternalStateUnavailable stand-in.
- The rearm is `Storage::replace_convergence_ledger` CAS; a failure replies NOT_RETIRED, closes the store and republishes the status so subscribers see Quarantined at once.
- Per-step replies come from `Reconciled.outcomes` (order zipped with steps); the reapply runs `reconcile_locked` with `Mode::Overwrite` scoped to the drifted subsystems.

## Tried and ruled out
- Asserting `publications().has_changed()` per reapply row: every mutation request publishes, so it cannot isolate the rearm republish. Read the published `active` convergence member after a NOT_RETIRED instead.
- Expecting firmware moving back to the saved preset to clear native drift: `thermal_drift::Tracker::external()` stays set until a reconciliation, so a reapply then reports Applied.

## Watch out
- `tests/data/thermal-reapply-vectors.json` is regenerated by `NativeThermalReapplyVectors` with OPENBLADE_REGENERATE_NATIVE_THERMAL_REAPPLY_VECTORS; the Rust replay counts rows (`replayed == 10`).
- The kbd+triggers lane has load-sensitive driver-mode-owner timing tests; at 100% host CPU they flake, and pass with `--test-threads=6` or alone.
