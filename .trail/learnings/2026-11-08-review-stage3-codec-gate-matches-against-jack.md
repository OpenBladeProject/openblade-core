## Decided
Stage3 deliberately replaces stage2's zero-test scaffold with real report tests and test-only serde parsing. Reviewed Trail check lexical findings against the actual delta: canonical vectors are read in place; expected values are never generated by production code; no new firmware grammar, admission, THX API/version, session or UI behavior is introduced. Historic stage1 statements that its PR adds no Rust workspace describe that earlier scope, not a prohibition on this authorized stage.

## Tried and ruled out
Do not treat Trail's vocabulary matches (production/runtime/vector, fixture/test/dependency, GPL3.0, host/model/status) as evidence that codec-only software has activated hardware or changed user-facing contracts. Keep the checker unchanged and disclose its nonzero lexical result in the PR.

## Watch out
Maintain no_std/unsafe compiler policies and inward registry-free production dependencies. Only pinned test parser roots and the two reviewed protocol test targets are newly admitted. Synthetic tests remain separate from physical evidence and no C ABI or product integration is part of stage3.
